Master Thesis

Through the eyes of a Vision-Language Model

Age-related Macular Degeneration analysis using fundus images

Probing visual latent spaces of multiple Vision-Language Models applied on retinal fundus images for Age-related Macular Degeneration. Statistical connection between AMD phenotypes and latent dimensions allows the implementation of three interconnected tasks:

  • Classification of current disease status through multiple downstream tasks, coupled with SHAP explainability;
  • Prediction of discrete future progression through multiple downstream tasks, coupled with SHAP explainability;
  • Forecasting of longitudinal trajectories from irregularly sampled data through multiple Neural Dynamical Systems, coupled with uncertainty quantification and explainability maps.
